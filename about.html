About

Elite coaching, for everyday people.

I'm Jordan Hale — the coach behind FORGE Personal Training in Norwich. Here's what I'm about, and why training with me is different.

My story

From rugby pitches to your corner

I spent ten years in rugby strength & conditioning, helping athletes get stronger, faster and more resilient. But the moments I loved most weren't the elite ones — they were watching everyday people surprise themselves: the first press-up, the first stone lost, the first time someone walked into a gym without dread.

So in 2017 I founded FORGE in Norwich with one mission: bring that same world-class coaching to real people with real lives. Busy parents, desk-bound professionals, complete beginners and seasoned lifters — everyone gets a plan built around them.

No fads. No shame. No one-size-fits-all programmes. Just honest, expert coaching that meets you exactly where you are.

How I coach

My training philosophy

  • 1Build the basics brilliantly. Master a handful of movements and the results take care of themselves.
  • 2Strength is the foundation. Getting stronger improves fat-loss, posture, energy and confidence — at any age.
  • 3Habits over heroics. Small, consistent changes beat punishing 6-week blitzes every time.
  • 4Make it enjoyable. The best plan is the one you actually look forward to and stick with.
