Best gyms & outdoor training spots in Norwich

By Jordan Hale · 12 May 2026 · 6 min read

Norwich is a brilliant place to get fit. Whether you love the buzz of a busy weights room, the calm of an early-morning run along the river, or a private studio where it's just you and your coach, the city has something for every kind of trainer. Here's my rundown — as a local personal trainer — of the best places to train across Norwich.

Best gyms for strength training

If lifting is your thing, you want plenty of barbells, racks and space to move. Norwich's dedicated strength and "big box" gyms around the city centre and the inner ring road are well-stocked and open early, which is perfect for before-work sessions. Look for somewhere with at least a few squat racks so you're never queuing during peak hours.

  • City-centre gyms — convenient if you work in town; busiest 5–7pm.
  • Riverside & Riverside Road — handy for parking and the train station.
  • Private PT studios (like FORGE) — no queues, no crowds, full coaching.
The best gym is the one you'll actually go to. Convenience beats fancy equipment nine times out of ten.

Best outdoor training spots

When the Norfolk weather plays ball, training outdoors is hard to beat. A few local favourites:

  • Mousehold Heath — trails, hills and open space; superb for running, intervals and bodyweight circuits.
  • Eaton Park — flat loops, an outdoor gym and plenty of room for mobility work.
  • The Riverside walk & Whitlingham — scenic, mostly flat routes ideal for steady runs and recovery walks.
  • Wensum Park — quiet and central, great for a quick lunchtime session.

Training around the city for free

You don't need a gym membership to get strong. A simple kettlebell, a set of resistance bands and one of Norwich's parks is enough for a genuinely effective programme. The key is structure and progression — doing the right things consistently, and gradually making them harder.
