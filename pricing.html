Good to know

Pricing FAQs

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first consultation — goal-setting, a movement screen and a plan — is completely free, with no obligation to continue.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Norwich?

It varies, but at FORGE you're looking at from £36 per 1-2-1 session in a block of ten, £25 for a single taster, £18 per person for small-group, and £99/month for online coaching. Always clear, never hidden.

Do you offer payment plans?

Absolutely. Monthly packages can be split into weekly or fortnightly payments to spread the cost — just ask at your consultation.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Reschedule any session with 24 hours' notice at no charge. Session blocks stay valid for 16 weeks.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes — vouchers are available for any package and make a brilliant gift. Drop me a message to arrange one.
