The beginner's strength plan: start lifting this week

By Jordan Hale · 10 Apr 2026 · 8 min read

Strength training is the single best thing most people can do for their health, shape and confidence — and it's far simpler than the internet makes it look. You don't need a complicated split or fancy machines. You need a few good movements, done well, a little more each week. Here's a plan you can genuinely start this week.

The principles

  • Train full-body, 3x a week (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri) with a rest day between.
  • Focus on the big movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull and carry.
  • Leave 1–2 reps "in the tank" on every set — technique before ego.
  • Progress gradually: add a rep or a little weight when a session feels easy.

The workout (do it 3x a week)

Three sets of each, resting 60–90 seconds between sets. Warm up first with 5 minutes of easy movement.

  • Goblet squat — 3 × 8–10 (legs & glutes)
  • Dumbbell Romanian deadlift — 3 × 8–10 (hamstrings & back)
  • Press-up or bench press — 3 × 6–10 (chest & shoulders)
  • Dumbbell row — 3 × 8–10 each side (upper back)
  • Plank — 3 × 20–40 seconds (core)
  • Farmer's carry — 3 × 20m (grip & whole body)
The secret to getting strong isn't intensity — it's showing up and slightly beating last week.

How to progress

This is where most beginners go wrong — they keep the workout identical for months. Instead, aim to do a little more over time: an extra rep, a slightly heavier dumbbell, a few more seconds on the plank. Keep a simple note on your phone of what you did, and try to nudge one thing forward each session. That's it. That's the whole game.

Staying safe

Good technique is everything — it's what keeps you progressing injury-free for years. If you're unsure whether a movement looks right, that's exactly where a coach earns their keep.
